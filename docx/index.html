About 1.0595

1.0595 is derived from the approximate frequency ratio of one semitone in twelve-tone equal temperament. Its visual identity emphasizes precision, restraint, and clarity. Deep navy represents stability and control, while muted steel blue is reserved for timing, focus, and active states.

關於 1.0595
1.0595 源自十二平均律中一個半音的近似頻率比。品牌以精確、克制與清晰為核心；深海軍藍象徵穩定與控制，低彩度鋼藍則專用於節拍、焦點與操作中的狀態。

Getting Started

Set the Tempo, choose a Subdivision preset, enable the required Tracks, and press Start. TAP estimates tempo from repeated taps. The metronome continues until Stop is pressed.

Split positions are decimal locations within one beat cycle. Enter values from 0 to less than 1, separated by commas, with up to three decimal places.

Tracks and Articulation

Expand one Track to edit its Pattern, Articulation, and Sound summary at the same information level. Click an event to cycle Normal, Tenuto, Accent, Marcato, and Staccato. Press and hold for 1.2 seconds to reset the event to Normal.

Sound Settings

Select Edit in the Track Sound row to open that Track directly in Settings. Sound selects a preset. Level, Pitch, and Length remain independent. Changing Brightness or Noise marks the preset as Custom. Audio changes are heard on the next event that has not already been scheduled.

繁體中文使用說明

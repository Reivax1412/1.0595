# 1.0595 — Concise CIS

## Brand idea

`1.0595` is derived from the approximate frequency ratio of one semitone in twelve-tone equal temperament. The identity emphasizes precision, restraint, control, and clarity.

## Wordmark

- Standard wordmark: `1.0595`
- Product version is not part of the logo.
- In the application header, place the version to the right at reduced size and contrast: `1.0595  v2.4.2`.
- Do not add music notes, waveforms, gradients, glow, or decorative symbols.

## Core colours

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Primary Navy | `#0A192B` |
| Background | Deep Navy | `#081522` |
| Surface | Secondary Navy | `#10243A` |
| Interaction | Elevated Navy | `#162D45` |
| Accent | Muted Steel Blue | `#6F9FBC` |
| Primary text | Off White | `#F2F4F5` |
| Secondary text | Cool Gray | `#B7C0C8` |
| Muted text | Steel Gray | `#84919C` |

Muted Steel Blue is reserved for focus, current selection, timing feedback, and primary actions. It should not become a large decorative background.

## Visual rules

- Use navy surfaces as the dominant visual field.
- Use high-contrast off-white text for primary values and labels.
- Use accent colour sparingly.
- Avoid multicolour Tracks, neon effects, glass effects, and large gradients.
- Distinguish hierarchy through spacing, weight, borders, and luminance before adding colour.
